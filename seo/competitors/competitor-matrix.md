## 2. Competitor Weaknesses & SEO Angles to Exploit

### 1. Smallpdf
- **Pain Points**: Aggressive upsells, strict 1-document-per-day limit on many tools, forced modal popups pushing annual subscriptions.
- **Winning Angle**: "Tired of Smallpdf daily limits? EditMyPDF is 100% free and unlimited, with zero server uploads."

### 2. iLovePDF
- **Pain Points**: Security concerns with uploading sensitive contracts or medical papers to cloud servers, queue delays on large files, rate limiting.
- **Winning Angle**: "The private alternative to iLovePDF that runs completely in your browser. Fast, zero latency, files never leave your device."

### 3. Sejda
- **Pain Points**: 3 tasks per hour limit, 200-page limit, slow document upload and download times.
- **Winning Angle**: "Edit existing PDF text without hour-long cooldowns or page limits."

### 4. Adobe Acrobat Online
- **Pain Points**: Heavy login wall, forces Adobe account creation, expensive recurring subscription for simple editing tasks.
- **Winning Angle**: "Fix a typo in your PDF in 5 seconds. No account, no Adobe subscription, 100% free."
